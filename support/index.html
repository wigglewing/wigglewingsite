How do I restore a purchase?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

What lighting works best for a scan?

Daylight or a bright room. Lay the glove flat, palm up.

Can GlutenScan replace medical advice?

No. It reads labels. Check with your doctor about your diet.

New packs are added in updates. The app shows what is coming next.
