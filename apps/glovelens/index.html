GloveLens

Scan your glove. Unlock your swing.

01

What it does

Your glove has been keeping notes on your grip. GloveLens reads them.

One photo

Lay your glove flat and take a picture. No sensors, no swing analyzer, no setup.

Wear-zone map

See where your glove is wearing, from palm and heel pad to thumb and fingers, and how hard.

Grip score

A score from 0 to 100, so you know where your grip stands at a glance.

Grip faults, ranked

The faults behind your score, most serious first, and what each one does to your ball flight.

Drills that fit

Coaching tuned to your hand, handicap and grip style.

Progress over time

Every report is saved. Scan again later to see whether the drills are working.

02

How it works

From glove to grip report in about a minute.

  1. 1

    Step 1 of 3

    Photograph your glove

    Palm side up, in good light. Use the camera or pick a photo from your library.

  2. 2

    Step 2 of 3

    GloveLens maps the wear

    It reads the friction pattern and matches it to known grip faults.

  3. 3

    Step 3 of 3

    Get your grip report

    Your score, wear zones, main fault and the drills to work on, saved to your history.

03

Questions

More help is on the Support page.

Do I need any special equipment?

No. Just your phone and the glove you already play in.

What lighting works best for a scan?

Daylight or a bright room. Lay the glove flat, palm up.

Can I use a photo I already took?

Yes. Pick it from your photo library instead of using the camera.

How long does an analysis take?

Usually well under a minute.

How does pricing work?

Each grip report uses one credit. Credits are bought in the app.

When does GloveLens launch?

Soon. Visit glovelens.com to get an email on launch day.
