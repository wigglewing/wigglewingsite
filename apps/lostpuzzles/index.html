How it works

Easy to learn. Hard to put down.

  1. 1

    Step 1 of 3

    Pick a puzzle

    Choose from easy, medium and hard grids. Solved and in-progress puzzles are marked.

  2. 2

    Step 2 of 3

    Fill in the regions

    Tap a cell to label it with its region’s number. Tap again to clear it.

  3. 3

    Step 3 of 3

    Check your work

    Check highlights mistakes without giving the answer away. Undo, erase or start over anytime.

Questions

More help is on the Support page.

What is Nanro?

A logic puzzle played on a grid split into bold regions. The rules:

  • Each number tells you how many cells in its region to label, and every label in that region is that number.
  • Every region has at least one labeled cell.
  • All labeled cells connect into one group.
  • No 2×2 block can be fully labeled.
  • Labeled cells next to each other across a region border must have different numbers.
Do I need an internet connection?

No. Every puzzle is built into the app, and it works fully offline.

Is there a timer?

There is a clock, but it is just for you. No leaderboards, no pressure.

Where do new puzzles come from?

New packs are added in updates. The app shows what is coming next.

Which puzzle types are coming next?

Nurikabe, Hitori, Heyawake and Shingoki.

Which iPhones does it run on?

Any iPhone running iOS 17 or later.
